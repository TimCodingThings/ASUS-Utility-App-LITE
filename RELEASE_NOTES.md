ASUS Utility LITE 1.2.10

- Adds bounded, local Profile diagnostics.log around manual profile commands and charger/resume restores: driver result, power source, ASUS/Armoury service states, and read-only temperature/fan/power endpoint samples before, immediately after, and approximately 2 and 8 seconds after an accepted command.
- Diagnostic failures never block controls, and no services are stopped or reconfigured. The log is not uploaded automatically.
- This is a diagnostic update for accepted-but-ineffective profile commands. It does not claim to fix charger profile persistence or establish reliable active-mode readback. Actual mode remains explicitly unverified.
- Retains the compact interface, fan-curve drafts, saved position, approved launcher, supplied logo, GPL-3.0 license, and G-Helper attribution.

Exit the previous tray copy and reopen the installed executable to load this update. After the charger settles, select Balanced and then Silent and wait at least eight seconds before collecting the log.
